\begin{abstract}
For a fixed fermion momentum cutoff and massless transverse photons,
exactly neutral trial states give
\(E^{\vac}_{\Lambda,L}\le-c_\Lambda L^5+C_\Lambda L^3\).
This disproves free-vacuum extensivity for that regulator; it does not
settle stability relative to the interacting vacuum. We prove finite-volume
self-adjointness, a matching lower-bound order, and extensive bounds with
either fixed infrared decoupling or a positive bare transverse photon mass.
These are specified cutoff models, not constructions of renormalized QED.

For the fully free-Wick-ordered, photon-free model, an explicit periodic
Coulomb--Kato estimate gives mean-field vacuum-relative stability
\(-C_\Lambda K\), including exactly neutral Slaters with pair coherence
and point nuclei, for \(\alpha<4/\pi\) and the stated range of box sizes.
The all-box-size assertion fails under both specified nuclear self-energy
conventions.

\smallskip\noindent\emph{Further deductions (Sections 13--16).}
Three next-step problems are resolved in their stated scopes.
First, a unit point nucleus and a single coherent pair give
\(E^{\rm HF}_{\Lambda,L}-E^{\rm HF,\vac}_{\Lambda,L}
\le m-c_\alpha\Lambda_f\) at fixed admissible volume.
Thus a cutoff-independent constant is impossible in this fixed-reference
normalization, even at physical coupling.
Second, exchange destabilizes the free sea at the static one-loop
photon-mass matching: exactly neutral product states lower its energy
by \(cL^3\). A strictly larger, exchange-dependent bare-mass threshold
is necessary for free-sea minimality, but is not proved sufficient.
Third, a canonical charge-sector comparison proves a genuine
vacuum-relative bound and a relative correlation bound for screened
lattice fermions, including pair terms and arbitrary correlated states.
That theorem requires neither a gap nor small coupling. Its constants
diverge as screening is removed. The subsequent primary paper
\emph{Vacuum-relative stability and charge gaps in cutoff QED} resolves the unscreened
many-body comparison in its positive-effective-mass regime, including
photons. Physical renormalization and a controlled low-energy reduction
remain unresolved.
\end{abstract}

This is a mathematical research memorandum, not a claim of a completed
nonperturbative construction of physical QED. Sections 1--8 preserve the
initial write-up; Sections 9--12 audit and develop the supplied continuation.
Sections 13--16 carry out further proof attempts and record three concrete
outcomes, with independent checks by the collaborating agents.
Appendices preserve the initial collaborating agents' technical
reports: conclusions, supporting arguments, source checks, and limitations.
The revised main text takes precedence where it sharpens those reports.
These reports are research deliverables, not private internal deliberations.
Statements proved directly below are distinguished from cited theorems and
from proposed extensions.
