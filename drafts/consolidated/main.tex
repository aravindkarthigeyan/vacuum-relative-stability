\begin{abstract}
For compact Hamiltonian $U(1)$ lattice QED with Wilson fermions,
nonnegative bare mass, untruncated electric rotors, and exact local
Gauss law, we prove $E_\nu\ge E_0+m|\sum_x\nu_x|$ at every positive
gauge coupling. Prescribed individual nuclear self-energy subtraction
then gives stability relative to the interacting vacuum, uniformly in
volume and source positions. The proof uses a positive centered
fermion trace, a positive electric heat kernel, and exact gauge
projection. At fixed lattice spacing we also prove the vacuum
thermodynamic limit, fixed-cell two-sided source bounds, and a
zero-boundary-flux thermodynamic limit for periodic source patterns.
The corresponding periodic-source limit remains open.

A frozen-link Schur-complement theorem gives a charge-extensive
nonrelativistic spectral error. Its Wilson kinetic mass and magnetic
moment require explicit matching; a bounded clover improvement
preserves the positivity method under a stated mass condition.
For dynamical links, smooth electric localization is controlled, but
the proposed source-relative bounds on bare flux tails and bare
pair number fail already in the vacuum. We prove those obstructions
and quantitative volume-dependent replacements.

A separate sharp-momentum-cutoff model admits canonical relative
stability with continuum point nuclei and quantized transverse
photons. Its ordering, effective-mass restrictions, photon limits,
and small-box obstruction are treated explicitly. No theorem here
constructs renormalized continuum QED, a dressed neutral pair gap,
atomic binding, or crystalline order.
\end{abstract}
