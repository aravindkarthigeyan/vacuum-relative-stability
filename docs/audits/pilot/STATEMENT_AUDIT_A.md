### 6.3 Kick-phase equivalence (source kick versus D1/D3 kick)

A source kick interval has `Φ = φ(t)⊗1` and `c = 0`. The spatial propagator is
`u_s = T-exp(−i∫φ)` with `det u_s = e^{−i∫trφ}`. Write `u_s = e^{iΘ}`. Then
`−∫trφ ≡ trΘ (mod 2π)`, so the source factor `e^{½∫TrA} = e^{2i∫trφ} = e^{−2i trΘ}`, which is the
D3/D6 factor (P5). Changing the logarithm moves `trΘ` by `2πk` and leaves `e^{−2i trΘ}` fixed.
Numerically, replacing `Θ` by `Θ + 2πP` (with `P` a rank-1 projector) changes `F` only at the
`10⁻¹²` relative rounding level.

## 8. Truth (refutation attempts) and non-vacuity

### 8.1 Mathematical check

I re-derived the source proof under the exact D1–D6 conventions and found no gap.
- Each free step is `E = e^{−τh}` with `h` Hermitian and `{h, C_5} = 0`, so `E*C_5E = C_5`.
  Kicks are unitary and commute with `C_5`. So `U*C_5U = C_5`, and the spectrum is invariant
  under `λ ↦ 1/λ̄` with multiplicity.
- `hβ + βh = 2(M⊗1)`, so `B = ⟨f, βf⟩` satisfies `B' = −2⟨f, Mf⟩ ≤ −2m_*‖f‖²` on free steps and
  is constant across kicks. This gives the annulus bounds and excludes `|λ| = 1`.
- `Tr h = 0` follows from the Clifford relations. So `F` is real, and the homotopy to `v = 0`,
  `M = m_*`, no kicks gives `F > 0`.
- T2 follows from `F(φ) = C∏|1+e^{iφ}λ_j|²` and Vandermonde's identity.

Edge cases:
- **ι = ∅** (allowed by `hd` with `d = 0`): all matrices are 0×0, `F = 1 > 0`, `charpoly = 1`,
  and `roots = 0`. T1 holds. In T2 only `q = 0` is allowed, `F ≡ 1`, and `1 ≤ 1·1·1`. True.
- **Zero-duration free steps** and **many or large kicks**: included in the random tests below.
