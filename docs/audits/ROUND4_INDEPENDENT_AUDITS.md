# Independent technical cross-audits — round four

These are shareable audit outcomes and checked arguments, not private
deliberations. The final manuscript is authoritative.

## Matching section

An independent reader checked the ordered Schur roots, whole-band
and low-energy error constants, exact same-link vacuum subtraction,
covariant-difference consistency estimate with noncommuting
derivatives, magnetic sign convention, Z-dependent scale arithmetic,
and generalized clover determinant argument. No substantive gap was

boundary-shell supports, nondecaying sharp-cutoff boundary norm,

the first homological equation. No mathematical correction was
required. A missing TeX command backslash and a control character
were corrected before final compilation.

## Bare excitation number and remainder section

An independent audit verified the exact uncompressed sea norm
3N/a^2, the compressed lower bound 3N/(2a^2), the singular-value
constant sqrt(eta^2+s^2)-eta<=s^2/(2eta), and Tr S^2=3N/a^2
for n>=3. It also checked the variational use of E0<=Eref and the
result 2Delta/m+6N/(ma)^2, local annihilation form bound, and
conditional Schur identity. The compressed counterexample now
explicitly uses the compressed vacuum energy E0,F.

## Thermodynamics section

An independent reader checked auxiliary cutting, the graph-degree
Wilson diagonal, retained cut-link rotors, the one-sided open-source
limit proof, arbitrary commensurate remainder boxes, exact constrained
cut-flux direct sum, and the conditional averaging argument. The doubled
incidence coefficient 2C_f and its positive-part remainder are correct.
The cut definition was clarified to include periodic closing links
whose endpoints belong to the same selected open block.

## Scope

These audits do not establish priority or replace review by an external
specialist. No full dynamical nonrelativistic theorem or unconditional
periodic-source thermodynamic limit is claimed. The exact outstanding
operator hypotheses appear in the paper.

A final read-only consistency audit checked the abstract, introduction,
disclosure, README, current report, and review/package notes. It found
no material inconsistency. The current report was sharpened to retain
m>0 for its excitation estimate, n>=3 for the vacuum obstructions, and
