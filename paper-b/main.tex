\begin{abstract}
We study a specified sharp-momentum-cutoff Hamiltonian with one massive
Dirac field, pair creation, quantized transverse photons, and static
continuum point nuclei. The Coulomb zero mode is omitted, total
neutrality is imposed, and the harmonic transverse photon mode is
fixed and omitted. For full four-field Wick ordering relative to the
free Dirac sea, positivity of an explicitly computed effective mass
gives canonical Gaussian domination and a charge gap. Completing
source squares and using heat-kernel electrostatics yields
$E(R,Z)-E^{\rm vac}\ge m_*\sum_iZ_i-C_\Lambda K$, uniformly in
admissible nuclear configurations and in volumes above a specified
lower bound. The photon proof includes trace-norm product convergence
and removal of a photon-coordinate truncation. Density-product
ordering gives a different Hamiltonian for which the positive-mass
condition is automatic at every finite cutoff.

These relative theorems coexist with explicit regulator obstructions.
The bare model with massless photons has vacuum trial energies of order $-L^5$;
an all-volume many-nucleus bound fails in small boxes; a fixed-reference
photon-free mean-field energy with one unit point nucleus decreases
linearly with the fermion cutoff; and exchange destabilizes the free
sea at the statically matched positive photon-mass coefficient.
A fixed positive bare transverse $A^2$ term restores an extensive
absolute lower bound, but neither its presence nor the static matching
calculation constructs renormalized massless QED. Proofs of these comparisons and counterexamples are included. No cutoff-uniform physical
renormalization, thermodynamic limit for the massless momentum
regulator, or dynamical nonrelativistic reduction is asserted.
\end{abstract}
